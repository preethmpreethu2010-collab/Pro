Explore Beautiful Karnataka

Karnataka is famous for its rich culture, delicious food, beautiful nature and history.

🍛 Famous Foods

Masala Dosa

A popular South Indian dish with crispy dosa and potato filling.

Mysore Pak

A famous sweet made using gram flour, ghee and sugar.

Ragi Mudde

A traditional Karnataka food made from finger millet flour.

🏞️ Famous Tourist Places

Mysore Palace

A beautiful historical palace in Mysuru.

Hampi

Famous for ancient temples and Vijayanagara architecture.

Jog Falls

One of India's famous waterfalls located in Karnataka.

🎭 Culture and Traditions

Yakshagana

A traditional dance-drama form popular in coastal Karnataka.

Kannada Language

Kannada is the official language of Karnataka.

Mysore Silk

Karnataka is famous for its beautiful Mysore silk sarees.

🎉 Famous Festivals

Mysuru Dasara

A grand celebration featuring cultural programmes and a famous procession.

Ugadi

Kannada New Year celebrated with traditional food and rituals.

Makara Sankranti

A harvest festival celebrated with traditional customs.
